PodAura brings podcasts, RSS reading, and audio/video playback together.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Organize RSS feeds into groups, search articles, and refresh automatically.</li>
<li>Play podcast episodes and local audio or video files.</li>
<li>Download episodes automatically or in bulk for offline playback.</li>
<li>Keep listening with background playback, a mini player, and Picture-in-Picture.</li>
<li>Adjust playback speed, choose audio and subtitle tracks, and set a sleep timer.</li>
<li>Create playlists and reorder your playback queue.</li>
<li>Browse and play episodes by date in the calendar.</li>
<li>Read full articles and translate them using your own translation API key.</li>
<li>Manage read status, favorites, and playlists in bulk.</li>
<li>Customize notifications by feed and group.</li>
<li>Import and export subscriptions with OPML.</li>
</ul>
